Unlike the rest of this toolbox, this tool can't run fully offline: your IP and location come from a third-party lookup service (ipapi.co or ipwho.is), and the latency checks talk directly to each target site — all straight from your browser, nothing routed through this site's own servers, but those third parties do see your IP, which is unavoidable for a tool like this.

Country/city, ISP, and other location details are estimates from IP-address databases, not ground truth — they can be wrong, especially for mobile carriers and corporate networks.
