Unlike the rest of this toolbox, this tool can't run fully offline: your IP and location come from a third-party lookup service (ipapi.co or ipwho.is), the VPN check calls two more (iplogs.com and ipapi.is), and the latency checks talk directly to each target site — all straight from your browser, nothing routed through this site's own servers, but those third parties do see your IP, which is unavoidable for a tool like this.

Country/city, ISP, and VPN/proxy flags are estimates from IP-address databases, not ground truth. Even dedicated VPN-detection services miss well-configured VPNs and residential proxies, and can misflag ordinary mobile or corporate networks — treat every result here as a signal, not a verdict.
