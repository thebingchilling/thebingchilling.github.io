Everything runs on your device — files are never uploaded anywhere. The output keeps your original file type unless you click the format-switch suggestion, which only ever offers a lossless swap to a more space-efficient format. The Audio tab uses a small (~0.6 MB) vendored MP3/Ogg Vorbis encoder (WAV needs none at all); dropping a PNG or WAV file also downloads a small vendored WebP or FLAC encoder (under 0.5 MB) to check whether that lossless switch is even worth suggesting — it's used for the check either way, whether or not you accept it.
