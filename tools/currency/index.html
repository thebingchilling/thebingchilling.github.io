shield

Like the IP tool, this one can't run fully offline: exchange rates come from a third-party rate service (open.er-api.com, falling back to frankfurter.app), and purchasing-power data comes from the World Bank's API — both called directly from your browser. Your main-currency choice is saved only in this browser's local storage — never uploaded anywhere.

These are reference exchange rates, not what you'll actually get — banks, ATMs, and currency counters add their own margin and fees on top. World Bank PPP figures are only published periodically (sometimes a year or two old) and aren't available for every currency — when that's the case, this tool falls back to showing the plain exchange-rate conversion instead.
